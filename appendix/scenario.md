# Basis for the scenario

[← Back to README](../README.md)

Ridgeview is fictional. The mission types, sensor payloads and operating concerns are modelled on two published Boston Dynamics case studies:

- **Wien Energie, Simmering power plant (Vienna, Austria).** Spot carries 360°, zoom RGB and thermal cameras plus gas sensors. It reads analog gauges, checks fire extinguishers and measures air quality near the boilers, and Wien Energie's own team built the AI models that interpret the images. Thermal imaging became its most important inspection tool. The plant also moved from five long routes to about 100 short mini-routes because the long routes were cumbersome to change.
  - [Spot Makes Austria's Largest Power Plant Safer](https://bostondynamics.com/case-studies/spot-makes-austrias-largest-power-plant-safer/)
  - [Spot Working at More Austrian Power Plants](https://bostondynamics.com/case-studies/spot-working-at-more-austrian-power-plants/)
- **J-POWER, Onikobe Geothermal Power Plant (Japan).** Six missions cover 188 measurement points: digitising field instrument readings, temperature, ultrasound anomaly detection, sound analysis, gas concentration and oil leaks. A stated goal is worker safety around hydrogen sulfide, by sending the robot in first.
  - [Spot at J-POWER](https://bostondynamics.com/case-studies/spot-at-j-power/)

The feedback items, tag numbers, readings and people are invented. Nothing here describes the real performance of Spot or of either plant.
